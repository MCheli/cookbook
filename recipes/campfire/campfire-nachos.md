## 🧀 Campfire Nachos

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 10 min (foil pan on coals or grill)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Base** | Tortilla chips | 1 large bag |
| **Protein** | Canned black beans, drained | 1 can (15 oz) |
| | Cooked chicken or leftover taco meat (optional) | 2 cups |
| **Dairy** | Cheddar or Mexican blend, shredded | 2.5 cups |
| **Toppings** | Jalapeños, sliced | to taste |
| | Salsa & sour cream | to taste |
| | Green onion, sliced | 2 |
| **Supplies** | Disposable foil pan | 1 (9x13) |
| | Foil, for covering | 1 sheet |

### Method
1. **Layer** – Spread half the chips in the foil pan. Scatter half the beans, meat, and cheese over them. Repeat with the rest.
2. **Cover** – Tent a sheet of foil loosely over the pan so the cheese melts without the chips scorching.
3. **Heat** – Set on the grill grate or the edge of the coals for 8–10 min. Rotate once. You want melted cheese, not a burnt bottom — keep it off direct flame.
4. **Top** – Uncover and add jalapeños, salsa, sour cream, and green onion.
5. **Eat** – Everyone eats from the pan. Throw the pan away and you're done.

### Grocery List
- **Pantry**: 1 large bag tortilla chips, 1 can black beans, salsa
- **Protein**: 2 cups cooked chicken or taco meat (optional)
- **Dairy**: 2.5 cups shredded cheddar or Mexican blend, sour cream
- **Produce**: Jalapeños, green onion
- **Supplies**: 1 disposable 9x13 foil pan, aluminum foil
