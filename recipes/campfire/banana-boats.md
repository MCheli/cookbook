## 🍌 Banana Boats

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 10 min (coals or grill)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Base** | Bananas, unpeeled | 4 |
| **Filling** | Mini marshmallows | 1 cup |
| | Chocolate chips | ½ cup |
| **Optional** | Peanut butter chips or crushed graham crackers | ¼ cup |
| **Supplies** | Heavy-duty foil | 4 sheets |

### Method
1. **Split** – Leave the banana in its peel. Slice lengthwise through the top peel and most of the way into the fruit, making a pocket. Don't cut through the bottom.
2. **Stuff** – Wedge marshmallows and chocolate chips down into the split, plus anything else you're adding.
3. **Wrap** – Wrap each banana in foil.
4. **Cook** – Set on coals or a grill grate, split side up, for 8–10 min until everything has melted.
5. **Eat** – Peel the foil back and eat it out of the skin with a spoon. Nothing to wash.

### Grocery List
- **Produce**: 4 bananas
- **Pantry**: Mini marshmallows, chocolate chips, peanut butter chips or graham crackers (optional)
- **Supplies**: Heavy-duty aluminum foil
