## 🌭 Foil Packet Sausage & Potatoes

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 25 min (campfire coals, grill, or 400°F oven)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Smoked sausage or kielbasa, sliced | 1 lb |
| **Vegetables** | Baby potatoes, halved | 1.5 lb |
| | Bell pepper, chunked | 1 |
| | Onion, chunked | 1 medium |
| **Seasoning** | Olive oil | 3 Tbsp |
| | Garlic powder | 1 tsp |
| | Paprika | 1 tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |
| **Supplies** | Heavy-duty foil | 4 large sheets |

### Method
1. **Toss** – Dump everything except the foil into a bag or bowl and shake until coated.
2. **Build Packets** – Pile a quarter of the mix onto each foil sheet. Fold the long edges together and crimp, then crimp the ends, leaving a little air inside.
3. **Cook** – Set on coals or a grill grate for 25 min, flipping once halfway. In an oven, 400°F for 30 min, no flipping.
4. **Check** – Open one carefully — steam escapes fast. Potatoes should slide off a fork.
5. **Eat** – Peel the foil back and eat straight out of the packet. Crumple and toss when done.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1 lb smoked sausage or kielbasa
- **Produce**: 1.5 lb baby potatoes, 1 bell pepper, 1 onion
- **Pantry**: Olive oil
- **Seasoning**: Garlic powder, paprika, salt, pepper
- **Supplies**: Heavy-duty aluminum foil
