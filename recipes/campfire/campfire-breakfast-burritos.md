## 🌯 Campfire Breakfast Burritos

**Servings:** 6 | **Prep:** 15 min (at home) | **Cook:** 10 min (coals or grill)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Breakfast sausage or bacon, cooked & crumbled | 1 lb |
| | Eggs, scrambled | 10 |
| **Base** | Large flour tortillas | 6 |
| **Vegetables** | Frozen diced hash browns | 2 cups |
| | Bell pepper & onion, diced | 1 each |
| **Dairy** | Cheddar, shredded | 1.5 cups |
| **Seasoning** | Salt & pepper | to taste |
| | Hot sauce | to taste |
| **Supplies** | Heavy-duty foil | 6 sheets |

### Method
1. **Cook at Home** – Brown the sausage, then the hash browns, pepper, and onion in the same pan. Scramble the eggs. Season everything.
2. **Roll** – Fill each tortilla with the mixture and cheese, fold in the sides, and roll tight.
3. **Wrap** – Wrap each burrito in foil. Refrigerate or freeze — they keep 3 days cold, a month frozen.
4. **Reheat at Camp** – Set the foil packets on warm coals or a grill grate for 8–10 min, turning once. Frozen takes about 15.
5. **Eat** – Unwrap the top and eat from the foil. No pans, no plates, no dishes at camp.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1 lb breakfast sausage or bacon, 10 eggs
- **Pantry**: 6 large flour tortillas, hot sauce
- **Frozen**: 2 cups diced hash browns
- **Produce**: 1 bell pepper, 1 onion
- **Dairy**: 1.5 cups shredded cheddar
- **Supplies**: Heavy-duty aluminum foil
