## 🌮 Walking Tacos

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 10 min (one skillet)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Ground beef | 1 lb |
| **Base** | Single-serving corn chip bags (Fritos or Doritos) | 4 |
| **Seasoning** | Taco seasoning | 1 packet |
| | Water | ⅓ cup |
| **Toppings** | Cheddar, shredded | 1 cup |
| | Lettuce, shredded | 1 cup |
| | Tomato, diced | 1 |
| | Sour cream & salsa | to taste |

### Method
1. **Brown** – Cook the ground beef in a skillet over the fire or camp stove until no pink remains, 8 min. Drain if you like.
2. **Season** – Stir in the taco seasoning and water. Simmer 2 min until it clings.
3. **Open Bags** – Crush the chips lightly in the bag, then cut or tear the bag open along one long side.
4. **Fill** – Spoon the beef right into the bag, then pile on cheese and toppings.
5. **Eat** – Eat it out of the bag with a fork. One skillet is the entire cleanup.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1 lb ground beef
- **Pantry**: 4 single-serving corn chip bags, 1 packet taco seasoning
- **Dairy**: 1 cup shredded cheddar, sour cream
- **Produce**: Lettuce, 1 tomato
- **Other**: Salsa
