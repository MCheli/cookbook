## 🥦 Instant Pot Cheesy Chicken, Broccoli & Rice

**Servings:** 4–6 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 10 min + pressure time

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Boneless chicken breasts, cubed | 1.5 lb |
| **Soup Base** | Cream of chicken soup | 1 can (10.5 oz) |
| | Chicken broth | 2 cups |
| **Grains** | White rice (long grain), rinsed | 1.5 cups |
| **Vegetables** | Onion, diced | 1 medium |
| | Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
| | Frozen broccoli florets | 2 cups |
| **Dairy** | Sharp cheddar, shredded | 1.5 cups |
| **Seasoning** | Salt & pepper | to taste |
| | Paprika | 1 tsp |
| | Butter | 1 Tbsp |

### Method
1. **Sauté** – Set the Instant Pot to sauté. Melt the butter, then cook the onion and garlic 2–3 min.
2. **Add Chicken** – Stir in the chicken, salt, pepper, and paprika. Cook 2 min.
3. **Layer** – Cancel sauté. Add the rice and broth and stir. Spoon the cream of chicken soup over the top without stirring.
4. **Pressure Cook** – Lock the lid and cook on HIGH pressure for 8 minutes.
5. **Release & Steam** – Quick release. Stir in the frozen broccoli, replace the lid (heat off), and let it steam 5 minutes.
6. **Cheese** – Stir in the shredded cheddar until melted and creamy. Serve hot.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1.5 lb boneless chicken breasts
- **Canned**: 1 can cream of chicken soup, 2 cups chicken broth
- **Grains**: 1.5 cups white rice
- **Produce**: 1 onion, garlic
- **Frozen**: Broccoli florets
- **Dairy**: 1.5 cups shredded sharp cheddar, butter
- **Seasoning**: Salt, pepper, paprika
