## 🥡 Instant Pot Beef & Broccoli

**Servings:** 4–6 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 10 min + pressure

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Flank or chuck steak, thin sliced | 1.5–2 lb |
| **Sauce** | Soy sauce | ½ cup |
| | Brown sugar | ¼ cup |
| | Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| | Ginger, grated | 1 tsp |
| | Cornstarch + water | 2 Tbsp each |
| **Vegetable** | Broccoli florets | 2 cups |

### Method
1. Add steak, soy sauce, sugar, garlic, and ginger to pot.
2. Cook HIGH pressure for 10 min, then quick release.
3. Stir in cornstarch slurry to thicken.
4. Steam or microwave broccoli, then stir in before serving.

### Grocery List
- 1.5–2 lb steak  
- Soy sauce  
- Brown sugar  
- Garlic  
- Ginger  
- Broccoli  
- Cornstarch
