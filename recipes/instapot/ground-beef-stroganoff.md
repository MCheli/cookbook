## 🍝 Ground Beef Stroganoff

**Servings:** 4–6 | **Prep:** 0 min | **Cook:** 6 min HP + sauté

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Oil** | Olive oil | 1 Tbsp |
| **Vegetables** | Onion, diced | 1 medium |
|  | Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
|  | Mushrooms, sliced | 8 oz |
| **Protein** | Ground beef | 1 lb |
| **Flavor** | Salt & pepper | to taste |
|  | Tomato paste | 1 Tbsp |
|  | Dijon mustard | 1 Tbsp |
|  | Worcestershire | 1 Tbsp |
|  | Dried thyme | 1/2 tsp |
| **Liquid** | Beef broth | 1.5 cups |
| **Pasta** | Egg noodles | 12 oz |
| **Finish** | Sour cream | 1 cup |
|  | Fresh parsley | 2 Tbsp |

### Method
1. **Sauté** – High heat, add oil. Cook onion, garlic, mushrooms with salt 5 min. Add beef, break up, cook 4 min. Stir in tomato paste, mustard, Worcestershire, thyme, pepper, broth, noodles.
2. **Pressure** – Seal lid. Cancel, then Manual HIGH 6 min.
3. **Release** – Natural 5 min, then vent. Stir in sour cream.
4. **Serve** – Top with parsley.

### Grocery List
- 1 lb ground beef, onion, 8 oz mushrooms, garlic, parsley  
- Tomato paste, Dijon, Worcestershire, thyme, olive oil  
- Beef broth, 12 oz egg noodles, sour cream
