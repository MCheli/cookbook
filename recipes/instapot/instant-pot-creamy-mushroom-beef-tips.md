## 🍄 Instant Pot Creamy Mushroom Beef Tips

**Servings:** 4–6 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 20 min + pressure time

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Stew beef or sirloin tips, cubed | 2 lb |
| **Soup Base** | Cream of mushroom soup | 2 cans (10.5 oz each) |
| | Beef broth | 1 cup |
| **Vegetables** | Mushrooms, sliced | 8 oz |
| | Onion, diced | 1 medium |
| | Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| **Seasoning** | Onion soup mix | 1 packet |
| | Worcestershire sauce | 1 Tbsp |
| | Black pepper | ½ tsp |
| **Serve With** | Egg noodles | 12 oz |
| **Finish** | Sour cream | ½ cup (optional) |

### Method
1. **Sauté** – Set the Instant Pot to sauté. Add oil and brown the beef in batches, 3–4 min per batch. Set aside.
2. **Aromatics** – Add the onion, mushrooms, and garlic. Cook 3 min, scraping up any browned bits.
3. **Combine** – Cancel sauté. Return the beef, then stir in the broth, Worcestershire, onion soup mix, and pepper. Spoon the cream of mushroom soup on top — do not stir it in, so it doesn't scorch.
4. **Pressure Cook** – Lock the lid and cook on HIGH pressure for 20 minutes.
5. **Release** – Natural release 10 minutes, then quick release the rest. Stir well.
6. **Finish** – Stir in the sour cream off heat and serve over cooked egg noodles.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 2 lb stew beef or sirloin tips
- **Canned**: 2 cans cream of mushroom soup, beef broth
- **Produce**: 8 oz mushrooms, 1 onion, garlic
- **Pantry**: 12 oz egg noodles, Worcestershire sauce, onion soup mix packet
- **Dairy**: Sour cream (optional)
- **Seasoning**: Black pepper
