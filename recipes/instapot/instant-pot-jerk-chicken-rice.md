## 🌶️ Instant Pot Jerk Chicken & Rice

**Servings:** 4–6 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 10 min + pressure time

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|---------|
| **Protein** | Boneless chicken thighs | 2 lb |
| **Seasoning** | Store-bought jerk seasoning | 3 Tbsp |
| | Brown sugar | 1 Tbsp |
| | Garlic powder | 1 tsp |
| **Rice** | Long grain rice | 1.5 cups |
| | Chicken broth | 2 cups |
| | Frozen mixed vegetables (optional) | 1 cup |
| **Finish** | Lime juice | 2 Tbsp |
| | Hot sauce (optional) | to taste |

### Method
1. **Season** – Rub chicken with jerk seasoning, brown sugar, and garlic powder.
2. **Dump & Layer** – Add rice and broth to pot. Place seasoned chicken on top (don't stir). Add frozen veggies if using.
3. **Pressure Cook** – Lock lid, cook HIGH pressure 10 minutes.
4. **Release & Finish** – Quick release. Drizzle with lime juice and add hot sauce if you want more heat.
5. **Serve** – Stir everything together and serve.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 2 lb boneless chicken thighs  
- **Seasoning**: Jar of jerk seasoning, brown sugar, garlic powder  
- **Pantry**: 1.5 cups rice, chicken broth, lime juice, hot sauce  
- **Optional**: Frozen mixed vegetables
