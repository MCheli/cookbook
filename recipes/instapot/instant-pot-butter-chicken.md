## 🍛 Instant Pot Butter Chicken

**Servings:** 4–6 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 10 min + pressure

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Chicken thighs, cut into chunks | 2 lb |
| **Sauce** | Tomato sauce | 1 can (15 oz) |
| | Heavy cream | ¾ cup |
| | Butter | ¼ cup |
| | Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| | Garam masala | 1 Tbsp |
| | Ground cumin | 1 tsp |
| | Ground ginger | 1 tsp |
| | Paprika | 1 tsp |
| | Salt | to taste |

### Method
1. Sauté garlic in butter using sauté mode.
2. Add chicken and spices, stir.
3. Add tomato sauce, cancel sauté, seal and cook HIGH pressure 10 min.
4. Quick release, stir in cream, and serve with rice or naan.

### Grocery List
- 2 lb chicken thighs  
- 1 can tomato sauce  
- Heavy cream  
- Butter  
- Garlic  
- Garam masala, cumin, ginger, paprika
