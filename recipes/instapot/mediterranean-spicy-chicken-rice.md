## 🌊 Mediterranean Spicy Chicken & Rice

**Servings:** 4–6 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 10 min + pressure time

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|---------|
| **Protein** | Boneless chicken thighs | 2 lb |
| **Seasoning** | Harissa paste (jar) | 2–3 Tbsp |
| | Garlic powder | 1 tsp |
| | Dried oregano | 1 tsp |
| **Rice** | Long grain rice | 1.5 cups |
| | Chicken broth | 2 cups |
| | Canned chickpeas, drained | 1 can |
| | Frozen peas (optional) | 1 cup |
| **Finish** | Lemon juice | 2 Tbsp |
| | Plain Greek yogurt (for serving) | 1/2 cup |

### Method
1. **Season** – Rub chicken with harissa paste, garlic powder, and oregano.
2. **Dump & Layer** – Add rice, broth, and chickpeas to pot. Place seasoned chicken on top (don't stir). Add frozen peas if using.
3. **Pressure Cook** – Lock lid, cook HIGH pressure 10 minutes.
4. **Release & Finish** – Quick release. Drizzle with lemon juice.
5. **Serve** – Stir everything together. Serve with a dollop of yogurt on top.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 2 lb boneless chicken thighs  
- **Seasoning**: Jar of harissa paste, garlic powder, dried oregano  
- **Pantry**: 1.5 cups rice, chicken broth, 1 can chickpeas, lemon juice  
- **Dairy**: Small container Greek yogurt  
- **Optional**: Frozen peas
