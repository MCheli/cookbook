## 🌮 Instant Pot Cheesy Taco Pasta

**Servings:** 6 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 15 min + pressure time

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Ground beef | 1.5 lb |
| **Soup Base** | Condensed nacho cheese soup | 1 can (10.75 oz) |
| | Beef broth | 3 cups |
| | Salsa | 1 cup |
| **Pasta** | Rotini or elbow macaroni | 12 oz |
| **Vegetables** | Onion, diced | 1 medium |
| | Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
| | Black beans, drained | 1 can (15 oz) |
| **Dairy** | Cheddar or Mexican blend, shredded | 1.5 cups |
| **Seasoning** | Taco seasoning | 1 packet |
| **Toppings** | Sour cream, cilantro, jalapeño | to taste |

### Method
1. **Brown** – Set the Instant Pot to sauté. Cook the ground beef with the onion until no pink remains, 6–8 min. Drain the excess fat.
2. **Season** – Stir in the garlic and taco seasoning and cook 1 min.
3. **Load** – Cancel sauté. Add the pasta, broth, salsa, and black beans. Stir so the pasta is submerged, then spoon the nacho cheese soup over the top without stirring.
4. **Pressure Cook** – Lock the lid and cook on HIGH pressure for 4 minutes.
5. **Release** – Quick release, venting in short bursts to avoid spitting. Stir well.
6. **Cheese** – Stir in the shredded cheese until melted. It thickens as it sits. Serve with sour cream, cilantro, and jalapeño.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1.5 lb ground beef
- **Canned**: 1 can condensed nacho cheese soup, 1 can black beans, beef broth, salsa
- **Pasta**: 12 oz rotini or elbow macaroni
- **Produce**: 1 onion, garlic, cilantro, jalapeño
- **Dairy**: 1.5 cups shredded cheddar or Mexican blend, sour cream
- **Seasoning**: 1 packet taco seasoning
