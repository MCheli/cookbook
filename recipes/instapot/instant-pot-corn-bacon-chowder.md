## 🌽 Instant Pot Corn & Bacon Chowder

**Servings:** 6 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 10 min + pressure time

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Bacon, chopped | 6 slices |
| **Soup Base** | Condensed cream of potato soup | 2 cans (10.5 oz each) |
| | Chicken broth | 2 cups |
| **Vegetables** | Frozen corn | 4 cups |
| | Yukon gold potatoes, diced | 1 lb |
| | Onion, diced | 1 medium |
| | Celery, diced | 2 stalks |
| | Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
| **Dairy** | Half & half or heavy cream | ¾ cup |
| **Seasoning** | Smoked paprika | 1 tsp |
| | Thyme, dried | ½ tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |

### Method
1. **Bacon** – Set the Instant Pot to sauté and cook the bacon until crisp, 6–8 min. Remove with a slotted spoon, leaving the fat behind.
2. **Aromatics** – Cook the onion, celery, and garlic in the bacon fat 3 min, scraping the bottom clean so nothing sticks.
3. **Load** – Cancel sauté. Add the potatoes, corn, broth, paprika, thyme, salt, and pepper. Spoon the cream of potato soup over the top without stirring.
4. **Pressure Cook** – Lock the lid and cook on HIGH pressure for 8 minutes.
5. **Release** – Quick release, then stir well to bring the soup base into the broth.
6. **Finish** – Stir in the cream off heat. Mash a few potatoes against the side for body. Top with the reserved bacon.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 6 slices bacon
- **Canned**: 2 cans condensed cream of potato soup, 2 cups chicken broth
- **Produce**: 1 lb Yukon gold potatoes, 1 onion, celery, garlic
- **Frozen**: 4 cups corn
- **Dairy**: Half & half or heavy cream
- **Seasoning**: Smoked paprika, dried thyme, salt, pepper
