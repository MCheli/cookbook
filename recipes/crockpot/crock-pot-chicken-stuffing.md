## 🍗 Crock Pot Chicken & Stuffing

**Servings:** 6 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 6 hr on LOW (3 hr on HIGH)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Boneless chicken breasts or thighs | 2 lb |
| **Soup Base** | Cream of chicken soup | 2 cans (10.5 oz each) |
| | Chicken broth | 1 cup |
| **Pantry** | Stuffing mix (chicken flavor) | 1 box (6 oz) |
| **Dairy** | Sour cream | ½ cup |
| **Vegetables** | Frozen green beans | 2 cups |
| **Seasoning** | Black pepper | ½ tsp |
| | Garlic powder | 1 tsp |

### Method
1. **Layer** – Place chicken in the bottom of the crock pot. Season with pepper and garlic powder.
2. **Mix Soup Base** – Whisk together the cream of chicken soup, broth, and sour cream. Pour over the chicken.
3. **Add Stuffing** – Sprinkle the dry stuffing mix evenly over the top. Do not stir.
4. **Cook** – Cover and cook on LOW 6 hr or HIGH 3 hr.
5. **Add Veggies** – Stir in the frozen green beans during the last 30 minutes.
6. **Serve** – Shred the chicken into the sauce and serve.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 2 lb boneless chicken
- **Canned**: 2 cans cream of chicken soup, chicken broth
- **Pantry**: 1 box chicken stuffing mix
- **Dairy**: Sour cream
- **Frozen**: Green beans
- **Seasoning**: Black pepper, garlic powder
