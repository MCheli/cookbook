## 🧀 Crock Pot Cheesy Chicken Spaghetti

**Servings:** 6 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 5 hr on LOW (2.5 hr on HIGH)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Boneless chicken breasts | 2 lb |
| **Soup Base** | Condensed cheddar cheese soup | 2 cans (10.75 oz each) |
| | Diced tomatoes with green chiles | 1 can (10 oz) |
| | Chicken broth | 1 cup |
| **Pasta** | Spaghetti, broken in half | 12 oz |
| **Vegetables** | Onion, diced | 1 medium |
| | Bell pepper, diced | 1 |
| | Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
| **Dairy** | Velveeta or shredded cheddar | 1 cup |
| **Seasoning** | Chili powder | 1 tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |

### Method
1. **Load** – Place the chicken, onion, bell pepper, and garlic in the crock pot. Season with chili powder, salt, and pepper.
2. **Soup Base** – Pour the cheddar cheese soup, tomatoes with chiles, and broth over the top.
3. **Cook** – Cover and cook on LOW 5 hr or HIGH 2.5 hr.
4. **Shred** – Pull the chicken out, shred it with two forks, and return it to the pot.
5. **Pasta** – Cook the spaghetti separately to al dente and drain. Stir it into the crock pot with the cheese until everything is coated and melted.
6. **Serve** – Let it sit 10 minutes on warm to thicken.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 2 lb boneless chicken breasts
- **Canned**: 2 cans condensed cheddar cheese soup, 1 can diced tomatoes with green chiles, chicken broth
- **Pasta**: 12 oz spaghetti
- **Produce**: 1 onion, 1 bell pepper, garlic
- **Dairy**: 1 cup Velveeta or shredded cheddar
- **Seasoning**: Chili powder, salt, pepper
