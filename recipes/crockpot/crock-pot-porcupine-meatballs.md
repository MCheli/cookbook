## 🍅 Crock Pot Porcupine Meatballs

**Servings:** 4–6 | **Prep:** 20 min | **Cook:** 6 hr on LOW (3 hr on HIGH)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Ground beef (80/20) | 1.5 lb |
| **Grains** | White rice, uncooked | ½ cup |
| **Soup Base** | Condensed tomato soup | 2 cans (10.75 oz each) |
| | Water | 1 cup |
| **Vegetables** | Onion, finely diced | 1 small |
| | Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
| **Binder** | Egg | 1 |
| **Seasoning** | Worcestershire sauce | 1 Tbsp |
| | Italian seasoning | 1 tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |

### Method
1. **Mix** – Combine the ground beef, uncooked rice, onion, garlic, egg, salt, pepper, and half the Italian seasoning. Don't overwork it.
2. **Roll** – Form into about 20 meatballs, roughly 1.5 inches each.
3. **Sauce** – Whisk the tomato soup, water, Worcestershire, and remaining Italian seasoning in the crock pot.
4. **Cook** – Settle the meatballs into the sauce in a single layer. Cover and cook on LOW 6 hr or HIGH 3 hr.
5. **Check** – The rice should be tender and the grains poking out — that's the "porcupine."
6. **Serve** – Spoon over mashed potatoes or eat as-is with bread.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1.5 lb ground beef
- **Canned**: 2 cans condensed tomato soup
- **Grains**: ½ cup white rice
- **Produce**: 1 small onion, garlic
- **Dairy**: 1 egg
- **Seasoning**: Worcestershire sauce, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper
