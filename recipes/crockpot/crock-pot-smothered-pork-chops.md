## 🐖 Crock Pot Smothered Pork Chops

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 6 hr on LOW (3 hr on HIGH)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Bone-in pork chops | 4 (about 2 lb) |
| **Soup Base** | Cream of mushroom soup | 2 cans (10.5 oz each) |
| | Chicken broth | ½ cup |
| **Vegetables** | Onion, sliced | 1 large |
| | Mushrooms, sliced | 8 oz |
| | Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| **Seasoning** | Onion soup mix | 1 packet |
| | Black pepper | ½ tsp |
| | Paprika | 1 tsp |
| **Finish** | Cornstarch + water | 2 Tbsp each (optional for thickening) |

### Method
1. **Sear (optional)** – Brown the pork chops 2 min per side in a hot skillet for extra flavor.
2. **Load** – Place the onion and mushrooms in the crock pot, then lay the chops on top. Season with pepper and paprika.
3. **Soup Base** – Whisk the cream of mushroom soup, broth, garlic, and onion soup mix together and pour over everything.
4. **Cook** – Cover and cook on LOW 6 hr or HIGH 3 hr, until the pork is fork-tender.
5. **Thicken** – Stir in a cornstarch slurry and cook 15 more minutes if you want a thicker gravy.
6. **Serve** – Spoon the gravy over mashed potatoes, rice, or egg noodles.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 4 bone-in pork chops (about 2 lb)
- **Canned**: 2 cans cream of mushroom soup, chicken broth
- **Produce**: 1 large onion, 8 oz mushrooms, garlic
- **Seasoning**: Onion soup mix packet, black pepper, paprika
- **Other**: Cornstarch (optional)
