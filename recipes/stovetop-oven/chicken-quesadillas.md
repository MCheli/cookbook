## 🧀 Chicken Quesadillas

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 15 min (stovetop)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Cooked shredded chicken (or rotisserie) | 2 cups |
| **Tortillas** | Large flour tortillas | 4 |
| **Cheese** | Shredded Mexican blend or cheddar | 2 cups |
| **Additions** | Bell pepper, diced | ½ |
| | Onion, diced | ½ small |
| | Cumin | ½ tsp |
| | Chili powder | ½ tsp |
| **Cooking** | Butter or oil | 1 Tbsp |
| **Serving** | Salsa, sour cream, guacamole | for dipping |

### Method
1. **Prep Filling** – Toss shredded chicken with cumin and chili powder. Optionally sauté bell pepper and onion until soft.
2. **Assemble** – Lay tortilla flat. Spread cheese on half, add chicken and veggies, fold in half.
3. **Cook** – Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat. Cook quesadilla 2–3 min per side until golden and cheese melts.
4. **Serve** – Cut into wedges and serve with salsa, sour cream, or guacamole.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: Rotisserie chicken or 2 chicken breasts
- **Tortillas**: Large flour tortillas (4 count)
- **Cheese**: Shredded Mexican blend or cheddar (8 oz bag)
- **Produce**: Bell pepper, onion
- **Spices**: Cumin, chili powder
- **Other**: Butter, salsa, sour cream, guacamole
