## 🍲 One-Pot Chili Mac

**Servings:** 4–6 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 20 min (one pot)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Ground beef | 1 lb |
| **Pasta** | Elbow macaroni, uncooked | 2 cups |
| **Canned** | Diced tomatoes | 1 can (14.5 oz) |
| | Kidney or chili beans, undrained | 1 can (15 oz) |
| | Beef broth or water | 2 cups |
| **Vegetables** | Onion, diced | 1 medium |
| **Seasoning** | Chili powder | 2 Tbsp |
| | Cumin | 1 tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |
| **Dairy** | Cheddar, shredded | 1 cup |

### Method
1. **Brown** – Cook the beef and onion in a large pot over medium-high heat until no pink remains, about 8 min. Drain the fat if you want.
2. **Dump** – Add the tomatoes, beans with their liquid, broth, macaroni, chili powder, and cumin. Stir once.
3. **Simmer** – Cover and cook 12–15 min, stirring every few minutes so the pasta doesn't stick. Splash in more liquid if it tightens up.
4. **Cheese** – Kill the heat, scatter the cheddar over the top, and put the lid back on for 2 min to melt.
5. **Serve** – Straight from the pot. The pasta cooks in the sauce, so there's nothing to drain.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1 lb ground beef
- **Pasta**: 2 cups elbow macaroni
- **Canned**: 1 can diced tomatoes, 1 can kidney or chili beans, beef broth
- **Produce**: 1 onion
- **Dairy**: 1 cup shredded cheddar
- **Seasoning**: Chili powder, cumin, salt, pepper
