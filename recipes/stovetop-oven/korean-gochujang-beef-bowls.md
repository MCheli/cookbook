## 🥢 Korean Gochujang Beef Bowls

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 15 min (one skillet)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Ground beef (85–90% lean) | 1 lb |
| **Sauce** | Soy sauce | 3 Tbsp |
| | Brown sugar | 2 Tbsp |
| | Gochujang (Korean chili paste) | 1–2 Tbsp |
| | Sesame oil | 1 tsp |
| **Aromatics** | Garlic, minced | 4 cloves |
| | Fresh ginger, grated | 1 tsp |
| | Green onions, sliced (whites and greens split) | 4 |
| **Base** | Microwave jasmine rice pouches | 2 (about 8.5 oz each) |
| **Toppings** | Sesame seeds | 1 tsp |
| | Cucumber, sliced | 1 |
| | *Optional* – Kimchi, fried egg | to taste |

### Method
1. **Mix Sauce** – Stir together the soy sauce, brown sugar, gochujang, and sesame oil. Start with 1 Tbsp gochujang if you're unsure; the heat builds as it cooks down.
2. **Brown** – Cook the beef in a large skillet over medium-high 6–8 min, breaking it up, until browned with some crispy bits. Drain off most of the fat, leaving about 1 Tbsp.
3. **Aromatics** – Push the beef to the side. Add the garlic, ginger, and green onion whites to the clearing and cook 30 sec until fragrant, then stir everything together.
4. **Glaze** – Pour in the sauce and simmer 2–3 min, stirring, until it cooks down and turns sticky on the beef.
5. **Rice** – Microwave the rice pouches per the package while the sauce reduces.
6. **Serve** – Beef over rice, topped with green onion greens and sesame seeds. Cucumber and kimchi on the side cool it down.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1 lb ground beef
- **Grains**: 2 microwave jasmine rice pouches
- **Produce**: garlic, fresh ginger, green onions, 1 cucumber
- **Pantry**: soy sauce, brown sugar, gochujang, sesame oil, sesame seeds
- **Optional**: kimchi, eggs
