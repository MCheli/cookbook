## 🍗 Skillet Chicken Parmesan

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 25 min (one oven-safe skillet)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Boneless chicken breasts, halved lengthwise | 4 (about 2 lb) |
| **Coating** | Italian breadcrumbs | 1 cup |
| | Parmesan, grated | ½ cup |
| **Sauce** | Jarred marinara | 2 cups |
| **Dairy** | Mozzarella, shredded | 1.5 cups |
| **Seasoning** | Olive oil | 3 Tbsp |
| | Garlic powder | 1 tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |
| **Finish** | Fresh basil | handful |

### Method
1. **Coat** – Mix the breadcrumbs, Parmesan, and garlic powder on a plate. Season the chicken, then press each piece into the crumbs on both sides. No egg wash, no dredging station.
2. **Sear** – Heat the oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high. Cook the cutlets 3–4 min per side until golden. They finish in the oven, so don't chase doneness here.
3. **Sauce** – Pour the marinara around the chicken, not over it, so the crust stays crisp on top.
4. **Cheese** – Scatter the mozzarella over the cutlets.
5. **Bake** – Slide into a 425°F oven for 10–12 min until the cheese is bubbling and the chicken hits 165°F.
6. **Serve** – Basil on top, straight from the skillet. Good over pasta or with bread.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 4 boneless chicken breasts (about 2 lb)
- **Pantry**: 1 cup Italian breadcrumbs, 2 cups jarred marinara, olive oil
- **Dairy**: Parmesan, 1.5 cups shredded mozzarella
- **Produce**: Fresh basil
- **Seasoning**: Garlic powder, salt, pepper
