## 🫛 Green Bean Casserole

**Servings:** 6–8 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 30 min (oven 350°F)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Soup Base** | Cream of mushroom soup | 2 cans (10.5 oz each) |
| | Milk | 1 cup |
| **Vegetables** | Green beans, frozen or canned, drained | 4 cups (about 2 lb) |
| | Mushrooms, sliced (optional) | 4 oz |
| **Topping** | French fried onions | 1 can (6 oz) |
| **Seasoning** | Soy sauce | 1 tsp |
| | Black pepper | ½ tsp |
| | Garlic powder | ½ tsp |

### Method
1. **Prep** – Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. **Mix** – In a 2-quart baking dish, stir together the soup, milk, soy sauce, pepper, and garlic powder until smooth.
3. **Combine** – Fold in the green beans, the mushrooms if using, and half the fried onions.
4. **Bake** – Bake uncovered 25 min until hot and bubbling.
5. **Top** – Scatter the remaining fried onions over the top and bake 5 more minutes until golden.

### Grocery List
- **Canned**: 2 cans cream of mushroom soup, 1 can French fried onions
- **Frozen/Canned**: 4 cups green beans
- **Produce**: 4 oz mushrooms (optional)
- **Dairy**: 1 cup milk
- **Seasoning**: Soy sauce, black pepper, garlic powder
