## 🌶️ Sheet Pan Chicken Fajitas

**Servings:** 4–6 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 20 min (oven 425°F)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Boneless chicken breasts or thighs, sliced | 2 lb |
| **Vegetables** | Bell peppers, sliced | 3 (mixed colors) |
| | Onion, sliced | 1 large |
| **Seasoning** | Taco or fajita seasoning | 1 packet |
| | Olive oil | 3 Tbsp |
| | Lime, juiced | 1 |
| **Serve With** | Flour tortillas | 8–10 |
| | Sour cream, salsa, cilantro | to taste |
| **Supplies** | Foil or parchment | 1 sheet |

### Method
1. **Prep** – Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan with foil or parchment — this is the whole cleanup strategy.
2. **Toss** – Pile the chicken, peppers, and onion on the pan. Drizzle with oil, sprinkle the seasoning over everything, and toss right on the pan with your hands.
3. **Spread** – Push everything into a single layer. Crowding steams it instead of roasting it, so use two pans if it's piled up.
4. **Roast** – Bake 18–20 min, tossing once halfway, until the chicken is cooked through and the pepper edges char.
5. **Finish** – Squeeze the lime over the top. Serve with warmed tortillas and toppings, then throw the foil away.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 2 lb boneless chicken breasts or thighs
- **Produce**: 3 bell peppers, 1 large onion, 1 lime, cilantro
- **Pantry**: 8–10 flour tortillas, olive oil, 1 packet taco or fajita seasoning
- **Dairy**: Sour cream
- **Other**: Salsa
- **Supplies**: Foil or parchment
