## 🫑 Sheet Pan Chicken Fajitas

**Servings:** 4–6 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 25 min (oven at 400°F)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Boneless chicken breasts, sliced | 1.5 lb |
| **Vegetables** | Bell peppers (assorted colors), sliced | 3 |
| | Onion, sliced | 1 large |
| **Seasoning** | Chili powder | 2 tsp |
| | Cumin | 1 tsp |
| | Garlic powder | 1 tsp |
| | Paprika | 1 tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |
| **Oil** | Olive oil | 2 Tbsp |
| **Serving** | Flour tortillas | 8 |
| | Lime wedges | 1 lime |

### Method
1. **Preheat** – Heat oven to 400°F. Line a sheet pan with foil or parchment.
2. **Season** – Toss chicken, peppers, and onion with olive oil and all seasonings.
3. **Spread** – Arrange in a single layer on the sheet pan.
4. **Bake** – Roast 20–25 min, stirring halfway, until chicken is cooked through.
5. **Serve** – Squeeze lime over top and serve in warm tortillas.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1.5 lb chicken breasts
- **Produce**: 3 bell peppers, 1 onion, 1 lime
- **Spices**: Chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, paprika, salt, pepper
- **Other**: Olive oil, flour tortillas
