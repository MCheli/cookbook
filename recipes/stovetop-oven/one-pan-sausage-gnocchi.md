## 🥔 One-Pan Sausage & Gnocchi

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 20 min (one skillet)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Italian sausage, casings removed | 1 lb |
| **Pasta** | Shelf-stable potato gnocchi | 1 lb |
| **Vegetables** | Baby spinach | 5 oz |
| | Cherry tomatoes, halved | 1 pint |
| | Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| **Dairy** | Heavy cream | ¾ cup |
| | Parmesan, grated | ½ cup |
| **Seasoning** | Italian seasoning | 1 tsp |
| | Red pepper flakes | ¼ tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |

### Method
1. **Brown** – Cook the sausage in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up, 6–7 min. Leave the fat in the pan.
2. **Gnocchi** – Add the gnocchi straight from the package — no boiling. Cook 4–5 min, stirring occasionally, until they're browned and crisp at the edges.
3. **Vegetables** – Stir in the garlic, tomatoes, and red pepper flakes. Cook 2 min until the tomatoes start to slump.
4. **Sauce** – Pour in the cream and Parmesan. Simmer 2–3 min until it coats everything.
5. **Wilt** – Add the spinach in handfuls, stirring until it collapses. Season and serve from the pan.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1 lb Italian sausage
- **Pasta**: 1 lb shelf-stable potato gnocchi
- **Produce**: 5 oz baby spinach, 1 pint cherry tomatoes, garlic
- **Dairy**: Heavy cream, Parmesan
- **Seasoning**: Italian seasoning, red pepper flakes, salt, pepper
