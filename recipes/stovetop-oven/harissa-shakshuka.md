## 🍅 Harissa Shakshuka

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 25 min (one skillet with lid)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Vegetables** | Onion, diced | 1 medium |
| | Red bell pepper, diced | 1 |
| | Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| **Sauce** | Crushed tomatoes | 1 can (28 oz) |
| | Harissa paste | 1–2 Tbsp |
| | Cumin | 1 tsp |
| | Smoked paprika | 1 tsp |
| | Salt | ½ tsp |
| | Olive oil | 2 Tbsp |
| **Protein** | Eggs (2 per person) | 8 large |
| **Finish** | Feta, crumbled | ½ cup |
| | Fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped | handful |
| **Serving** | Crusty bread or warm pita | for dipping |

### Method
1. **Soften** – Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium. Cook the onion and pepper 6–8 min until soft. Add the garlic, cumin, paprika, and harissa, and cook 1 min.
2. **Simmer** – Stir in the tomatoes and salt. Simmer 8–10 min, stirring now and then, until thick enough that a spoon leaves a trail. Taste and add more harissa if you want more heat.
3. **Eggs** – Make 8 wells in the sauce with a spoon and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook on medium-low 5–8 min until the whites are set and the yolks are still runny.
4. **Finish** – Scatter the feta and herbs over the top.
5. **Serve** – Straight from the skillet with bread for scooping up the sauce.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 8 eggs
- **Canned**: 1 can crushed tomatoes (28 oz)
- **Produce**: 1 onion, 1 red bell pepper, garlic, parsley or cilantro
- **Dairy**: feta
- **Pantry**: harissa paste, olive oil
- **Spices**: cumin, smoked paprika, salt
- **Bread**: crusty bread or pita
