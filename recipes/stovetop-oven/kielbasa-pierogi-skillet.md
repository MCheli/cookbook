## 🥟 Kielbasa & Pierogi Skillet

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 20 min (one skillet)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Smoked kielbasa rope (fully cooked), sliced into coins | 1 rope (12–14 oz) |
| **Pierogies** | Frozen potato & cheese pierogies | 1 package (about 16 oz) |
| **Vegetables** | Onion, sliced | 1 large |
| **Cooking** | Butter | 3 Tbsp |
| | Water | ¼ cup |
| **Seasoning** | Smoked paprika | ½ tsp |
| | Garlic powder | ½ tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |
| **Serving** | Sour cream | ½ cup |
| | Spicy brown or grainy mustard | for dipping |

### Method
1. **Brown Kielbasa** – Cook the kielbasa coins in a large skillet over medium-high 4–5 min until browned on both sides. Move to a plate, leaving the drippings.
2. **Onions** – Add 1 Tbsp butter and the onion. Cook 5–6 min until soft and starting to brown. Add to the plate with the kielbasa.
3. **Pierogies** – Melt the remaining 2 Tbsp butter and add the pierogies straight from frozen in a single layer. Pour in the water, cover, and steam 4–5 min. Uncover and cook 2–3 min per side until golden and the water is gone. If they don't all fit, do two batches.
4. **Combine** – Return the kielbasa and onions to the pan. Sprinkle with smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper, and toss 1–2 min to heat through.
5. **Serve** – Straight from the skillet with sour cream and mustard for dipping.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1 smoked kielbasa rope (12–14 oz)
- **Frozen**: 1 package potato & cheese pierogies (about 16 oz)
- **Produce**: 1 large onion
- **Dairy**: butter, sour cream
- **Pantry**: smoked paprika, garlic powder, spicy brown or grainy mustard
