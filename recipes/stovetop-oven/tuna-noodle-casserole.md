## 🐟 Tuna Noodle Casserole

**Servings:** 6 | **Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 30 min (oven 375°F)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Canned tuna, drained | 2 cans (5 oz each) |
| **Soup Base** | Cream of mushroom soup | 2 cans (10.5 oz each) |
| | Milk | ¾ cup |
| **Pasta** | Egg noodles | 12 oz |
| **Vegetables** | Frozen peas | 1 cup |
| | Onion, diced | ½ medium |
| | Celery, diced | 2 stalks |
| **Dairy** | Cheddar cheese, shredded | 1 cup |
| **Topping** | Panko or crushed potato chips | 1 cup |
| | Butter, melted | 2 Tbsp |
| **Seasoning** | Salt & pepper | to taste |

### Method
1. **Prep** – Heat the oven to 375°F. Boil the egg noodles 1 minute short of package directions, then drain.
2. **Sauté** – Cook the onion and celery in a little butter over medium heat until soft, about 5 min.
3. **Mix** – In a large bowl combine the soup, milk, tuna, peas, sautéed vegetables, ½ cup of the cheddar, salt, pepper, and the drained noodles.
4. **Assemble** – Spread into a greased 9x13 dish. Top with the remaining cheddar, then the panko tossed with melted butter.
5. **Bake** – Bake 25–30 min until bubbly and the top is golden.
6. **Rest** – Let it sit 5 minutes before serving.

### Grocery List
- **Canned**: 2 cans tuna, 2 cans cream of mushroom soup
- **Pasta**: 12 oz egg noodles
- **Produce**: 1 onion, celery
- **Frozen**: Peas
- **Dairy**: Milk, 1 cup shredded cheddar, butter
- **Pantry**: Panko breadcrumbs or potato chips
- **Seasoning**: Salt, pepper
