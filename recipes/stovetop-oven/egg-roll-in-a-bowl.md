## 🥬 Egg Roll in a Bowl

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 15 min (one skillet)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Ground pork or turkey | 1 lb |
| **Vegetables** | Coleslaw mix (bagged) | 1 bag (14 oz) |
| | Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| | Fresh ginger, grated | 1 Tbsp |
| | Green onions, sliced | 4 |
| **Sauce** | Soy sauce | 3 Tbsp |
| | Sesame oil | 1 Tbsp |
| | Rice vinegar | 1 Tbsp |
| | Sriracha | 1 tsp (optional) |
| **Finish** | Sesame seeds | 1 Tbsp |

### Method
1. **Brown** – Cook the ground pork in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up, until no pink remains, 6–7 min. Drain if there's a lot of fat.
2. **Aromatics** – Stir in the garlic and ginger and cook 1 min until fragrant.
3. **Slaw** – Dump in the whole bag of coleslaw mix. It looks like too much; it cooks down in about 4 min.
4. **Sauce** – Add the soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, and sriracha. Toss 2 min more until the cabbage is tender-crisp.
5. **Serve** – Top with green onions and sesame seeds. Eat as-is, or over rice if you want it to stretch further.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1 lb ground pork or turkey
- **Produce**: 1 bag coleslaw mix (14 oz), garlic, fresh ginger, green onions
- **Pantry**: Soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, sriracha, sesame seeds
