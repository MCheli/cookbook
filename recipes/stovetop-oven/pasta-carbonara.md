## 🍝 Pasta Carbonara

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 20 min (stovetop)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Pasta** | Spaghetti or linguine | 1 lb |
| **Protein** | Bacon or pancetta, diced | 8 oz |
| **Sauce** | Eggs | 3 large |
| | Parmesan cheese, grated | 1 cup |
| | Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
| **Seasoning** | Black pepper | 1 tsp (generous) |
| | Salt | to taste |
| **Pasta Water** | Reserved | ½ cup |

### Method
1. **Cook Pasta** – Boil pasta in salted water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water before draining.
2. **Cook Bacon** – While pasta cooks, fry bacon in a large skillet until crispy. Add garlic, cook 30 sec.
3. **Make Sauce** – Whisk eggs and parmesan together in a bowl.
4. **Combine** – Remove skillet from heat. Add hot pasta to bacon. Quickly toss in egg mixture, stirring constantly (the residual heat cooks the eggs). Add pasta water as needed for creaminess.
5. **Season & Serve** – Add plenty of black pepper, toss, and serve immediately.

### Grocery List
- **Pasta**: 1 lb spaghetti or linguine
- **Protein**: 8 oz bacon or pancetta
- **Dairy**: 3 eggs, parmesan cheese (wedge or pre-grated)
- **Produce**: Garlic
- **Spices**: Black pepper
