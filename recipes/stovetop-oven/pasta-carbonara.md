## 🍝 Pasta with Sausage Cream Sauce

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 20 min (stovetop)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Pasta** | Penne or rigatoni | 1 lb |
| **Protein** | Italian sausage links, sliced into coins | 12 oz |
| **Sauce** | Heavy cream | 1 cup |
| | Parmesan cheese, grated | ½ cup |
| | Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| | Crushed tomatoes | ½ cup |
| **Seasoning** | Italian seasoning | 1 tsp |
| | Red pepper flakes | ½ tsp |
| | Salt & black pepper | to taste |
| **Pasta Water** | Reserved | ½ cup |

### Method
1. **Cook Pasta** – Boil pasta in salted water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water before draining.
2. **Brown Sausage** – While pasta cooks, heat a large skillet over medium-high. Brown sliced sausage 3–4 min per side until cooked through. Set aside, leaving drippings in pan.
3. **Build Sauce** – Reduce heat to medium. Add garlic to the skillet, cook 30 sec. Stir in crushed tomatoes and cook 2 min. Pour in cream and bring to a gentle simmer.
4. **Finish** – Add parmesan, Italian seasoning, and red pepper flakes. Stir until smooth. Return sausage to the pan.
5. **Combine** – Toss in drained pasta, adding pasta water as needed to loosen the sauce.
6. **Season & Serve** – Taste, adjust salt and pepper, and serve immediately with extra parmesan.

### Grocery List
- **Pasta**: 1 lb penne or rigatoni
- **Protein**: 12 oz Italian sausage links (mild or hot)
- **Dairy**: heavy cream, parmesan cheese (wedge or pre-grated)
- **Canned**: crushed tomatoes
- **Produce**: Garlic
- **Spices**: Italian seasoning, red pepper flakes
