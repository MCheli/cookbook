## 🍳 Egg Fried Rice

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 15 min (stovetop)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Base** | Cooked rice (day-old preferred) | 4 cups |
| **Protein** | Eggs | 3 large |
| **Aromatics** | Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| | Green onions, sliced | 4 |
| **Sauce** | Soy sauce | 3 Tbsp |
| | Sesame oil | 1 tsp |
| | Oyster sauce (optional) | 1 Tbsp |
| **Vegetables** | Frozen peas & carrots | 1 cup |
| **Cooking** | Vegetable oil | 2 Tbsp |
| **Seasoning** | Salt & white pepper | to taste |

### Method
1. **Prep** – Have all ingredients ready before you start — this cooks fast. Day-old cold rice works best; fresh rice can be used but will be stickier.
2. **Scramble Eggs** – Heat 1 Tbsp oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Add beaten eggs, scramble until just set, then push to the side.
3. **Cook Aromatics** – Add remaining oil to the pan. Cook garlic and white parts of green onion 30 sec.
4. **Add Veggies** – Toss in frozen peas and carrots, stir-fry 2 min until heated through.
5. **Fry Rice** – Add rice and press into the pan. Let it sit undisturbed 1–2 min to get a little crispy, then toss everything together.
6. **Season** – Drizzle soy sauce, sesame oil, and oyster sauce over the rice. Toss to coat evenly.
7. **Serve** – Top with green parts of green onion.

### Grocery List
- **Grains**: 4 cups cooked rice (or 2 cups dry to cook ahead)
- **Protein**: 3 eggs
- **Frozen**: peas & carrots mix
- **Produce**: garlic, green onions
- **Pantry**: soy sauce, sesame oil, oyster sauce (optional), vegetable oil
