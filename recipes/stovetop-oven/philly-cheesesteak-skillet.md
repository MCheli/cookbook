## 🥖 Philly Cheesesteak Skillet

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 15 min (one skillet)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Shaved steak (thin-sliced ribeye or sirloin) | 1.5 lb |
| **Vegetables** | Onion, thinly sliced | 1 large |
| | Green bell pepper, sliced | 1 |
| | *Optional* – Mushrooms, sliced | 8 oz |
| **Cheese** | Provolone, sliced (or white American) | 8 slices |
| **Bread** | Hoagie rolls, split | 4 |
| **Seasoning** | Vegetable oil | 2 Tbsp |
| | Worcestershire sauce | 1 Tbsp |
| | Garlic powder | ½ tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |
| **Serving** | *Optional* – Hot cherry peppers or pickled jalapeños | to taste |

### Method
1. **Toast Rolls** – Toast the rolls cut-side down in the dry skillet over medium 1–2 min. Set aside.
2. **Veggies** – Add 1 Tbsp oil and turn the heat to medium-high. Cook the onion, pepper, and mushrooms 6–8 min until soft and browned at the edges. Season with salt and move to a plate.
3. **Steak** – Add the remaining oil and turn the heat to high. Spread out the steak, season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder, and cook 3–4 min, chopping it up with the spatula, until no pink remains. Splash in the Worcestershire.
4. **Combine** – Stir the veggies back in, then push the mixture into 4 roll-sized piles.
5. **Cheese** – Lay 2 slices of provolone over each pile. Cover 1–2 min until melted.
6. **Serve** – Slide a spatula under each pile and flip it into a roll. Top with hot peppers if you want the kick.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1.5 lb shaved steak (ribeye or sirloin)
- **Produce**: 1 large onion, 1 green bell pepper, 8 oz mushrooms (optional)
- **Dairy**: 8 slices provolone (or white American)
- **Bread**: 4 hoagie rolls
- **Pantry**: vegetable oil, Worcestershire sauce, garlic powder, hot cherry peppers or pickled jalapeños (optional)
