## 🥔 Ham & Scalloped Potato Bake

**Servings:** 6 | **Prep:** 20 min | **Cook:** 1 hr 15 min (oven 375°F)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Cooked ham, diced | 2 cups |
| **Soup Base** | Condensed cream of celery soup | 2 cans (10.5 oz each) |
| | Milk | 1 cup |
| **Vegetables** | Russet potatoes, thinly sliced | 2.5 lb |
| | Onion, thinly sliced | 1 medium |
| **Dairy** | Sharp cheddar, shredded | 1.5 cups |
| | Butter | 2 Tbsp |
| **Seasoning** | Dijon mustard | 1 Tbsp |
| | Black pepper | ½ tsp |
| | Paprika | ½ tsp |

### Method
1. **Prep** – Heat the oven to 375°F and butter a 9x13 dish. Slice the potatoes about ⅛ inch thick — a mandoline helps.
2. **Sauce** – Whisk the cream of celery soup, milk, Dijon, and pepper together until smooth.
3. **Layer** – Shingle a third of the potatoes in the dish, then a third of the onion, ham, sauce, and cheese. Repeat twice, finishing with cheese and a dusting of paprika.
4. **Bake Covered** – Cover tightly with foil and bake 45 min.
5. **Bake Uncovered** – Uncover and bake 25–30 min more until the potatoes are tender to a knife and the top is browned.
6. **Rest** – Let it stand 10 minutes so the sauce sets before serving.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 2 cups diced cooked ham (or a ham steak)
- **Canned**: 2 cans condensed cream of celery soup
- **Produce**: 2.5 lb russet potatoes, 1 onion
- **Dairy**: 1 cup milk, 1.5 cups shredded sharp cheddar, butter
- **Seasoning**: Dijon mustard, black pepper, paprika
