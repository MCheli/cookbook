## 🍯 Hot Honey Pork Chops & Green Beans

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 20 min (one skillet)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Boneless pork chops, about 1 inch thick | 4 (about 1.5–2 lb) |
| **Rub** | Smoked paprika | 1 tsp |
| | Garlic powder | 1 tsp |
| | Salt | 1 tsp |
| | Black pepper | ½ tsp |
| **Glaze** | Hot honey (e.g. Mike's) | ¼ cup |
| | Butter | 2 Tbsp |
| | Apple cider vinegar | 1 Tbsp |
| | *Optional* – Red pepper flakes | pinch |
| **Vegetables** | Green beans, trimmed | 1 lb |
| | Water | ¼ cup |
| **Cooking** | Vegetable oil | 1 Tbsp |

### Method
1. **Season** – Pat the chops dry. Mix the paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper, and rub it over both sides.
2. **Sear** – Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Cook the chops 4–5 min per side until browned and 145°F in the center. Move to a plate to rest.
3. **Green Beans** – Turn the heat to medium. Add the green beans and water to the same pan, scraping up the browned bits. Cover and steam 4–5 min until bright green and crisp-tender. Uncover, let any water cook off, salt lightly, and move to plates.
4. **Glaze** – Add the butter, hot honey, and vinegar to the pan. Bubble 1 min until slightly thickened; stay with it, since honey scorches fast. Return the chops and their juices and turn to coat.
5. **Serve** – Chops next to the green beans, with the leftover glaze spooned over both.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 4 boneless pork chops, about 1 inch thick (1.5–2 lb)
- **Produce**: 1 lb green beans (pre-trimmed bag saves time)
- **Dairy**: butter
- **Pantry**: hot honey, apple cider vinegar, vegetable oil
- **Spices**: smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, black pepper, red pepper flakes (optional)
