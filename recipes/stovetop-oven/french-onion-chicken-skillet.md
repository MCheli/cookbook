## 🧅 French Onion Chicken Skillet

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 30 min (stovetop + broiler)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Boneless chicken breasts or thighs | 4 (about 2 lb) |
| **Soup Base** | Condensed French onion soup | 1 can (10.5 oz) |
| | Beef broth | ½ cup |
| **Vegetables** | Onion, thinly sliced | 2 large |
| | Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| **Dairy** | Gruyère or Swiss cheese, shredded | 1.5 cups |
| | Butter | 2 Tbsp |
| **Seasoning** | Fresh thyme (or ½ tsp dried) | 1 tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |
| | Flour | 2 Tbsp |

### Method
1. **Sear** – Season the chicken with salt and pepper. Brown 4 min per side in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Set aside.
2. **Caramelize** – Melt the butter, add the sliced onions and a pinch of salt. Cook 12–15 min, stirring, until deep golden.
3. **Sauce** – Stir in the garlic and thyme for 1 min, then the flour for another minute. Add the French onion soup and broth, scraping the pan. Simmer 5 min until thickened.
4. **Return Chicken** – Nestle the chicken back into the sauce along with any juices.
5. **Melt** – Blanket with the shredded cheese and broil 3–4 min until bubbly and browned.
6. **Serve** – Spoon the onions over the top. Good with crusty bread or mashed potatoes.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 4 boneless chicken breasts or thighs (about 2 lb)
- **Canned**: 1 can condensed French onion soup, beef broth
- **Produce**: 2 large onions, garlic, fresh thyme
- **Dairy**: 1.5 cups shredded Gruyère or Swiss, butter
- **Pantry**: Flour
- **Seasoning**: Salt, pepper
