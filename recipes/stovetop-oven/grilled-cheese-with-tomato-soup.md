## 🥪 Grilled Cheese with Tomato Soup

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 20 min (stovetop)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Sandwich** | Bread slices | 8 |
| | Cheddar cheese, sliced | 8 slices |
| | Butter, softened | 4 Tbsp |
| **Soup** | Canned crushed tomatoes | 1 can (28 oz) |
| | Chicken or vegetable broth | 1 cup |
| | Heavy cream | ¼ cup |
| | Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
| | Butter | 1 Tbsp |
| | Salt, pepper, sugar | to taste |
| | Fresh basil (optional) | for garnish |

### Method
1. **Start Soup** – Melt 1 Tbsp butter in a pot over medium heat. Sauté garlic 1 min. Add tomatoes, broth, salt, pepper, and a pinch of sugar. Simmer 15 min. Stir in cream.
2. **Make Sandwiches** – Butter one side of each bread slice. Place cheese between two slices (butter sides out).
3. **Grill** – Cook sandwiches in a skillet over medium heat, 3–4 min per side until golden and cheese melts.
4. **Serve** – Cut sandwiches diagonally. Serve with a bowl of soup for dipping.

### Grocery List
- **Bread**: 1 loaf sliced bread
- **Cheese**: 8 oz sliced cheddar
- **Dairy**: Butter, heavy cream
- **Canned**: 1 can crushed tomatoes (28 oz), chicken or vegetable broth
- **Produce**: Garlic, fresh basil (optional)
