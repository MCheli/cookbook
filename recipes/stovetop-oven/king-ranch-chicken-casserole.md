## 🌶️ King Ranch Chicken Casserole

**Servings:** 6–8 | **Prep:** 20 min | **Cook:** 35 min (oven 350°F)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Cooked chicken, shredded (rotisserie works) | 4 cups |
| **Soup Base** | Cream of mushroom soup | 1 can (10.5 oz) |
| | Cream of chicken soup | 1 can (10.5 oz) |
| | Diced tomatoes with green chiles | 1 can (10 oz) |
| | Chicken broth | ½ cup |
| **Vegetables** | Onion, diced | 1 medium |
| | Bell pepper, diced | 1 |
| | Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
| **Tortillas** | Corn tortillas, cut into strips | 10–12 |
| **Dairy** | Cheddar & Monterey Jack, shredded | 2 cups |
| **Seasoning** | Chili powder | 1 tsp |
| | Cumin | 1 tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |

### Method
1. **Prep** – Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9x13 dish.
2. **Sauté** – Cook the onion, bell pepper, and garlic in a skillet over medium heat until soft, 5 min.
3. **Sauce** – Stir in both soups, the tomatoes with chiles, broth, chili powder, cumin, salt, and pepper. Simmer 3 min, then fold in the chicken.
4. **Layer** – Spread a third of the tortilla strips in the dish, top with a third of the chicken mixture and a handful of cheese. Repeat twice, finishing with cheese.
5. **Bake** – Cover with foil and bake 25 min, then uncover and bake 10 more until bubbly and browned.
6. **Rest** – Let it rest 10 minutes so it sets before cutting.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1 rotisserie chicken (or 4 cups cooked chicken)
- **Canned**: 1 can cream of mushroom soup, 1 can cream of chicken soup, 1 can diced tomatoes with green chiles, chicken broth
- **Produce**: 1 onion, 1 bell pepper, garlic
- **Pantry**: 10–12 corn tortillas
- **Dairy**: 2 cups shredded cheddar & Monterey Jack
- **Seasoning**: Chili powder, cumin, salt, pepper
