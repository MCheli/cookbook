## 🍅 One-Pot Creamy Tomato Pasta

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 20 min (one pot)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Pasta** | Penne or rigatoni, uncooked | 12 oz |
| **Canned** | Crushed tomatoes | 1 can (28 oz) |
| | Chicken or vegetable broth | 2 cups |
| **Vegetables** | Onion, diced | 1 medium |
| | Garlic, minced | 4 cloves |
| **Dairy** | Heavy cream | ½ cup |
| | Parmesan, grated | ½ cup |
| | Butter | 2 Tbsp |
| **Seasoning** | Italian seasoning | 1 tsp |
| | Red pepper flakes | ¼ tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |
| **Finish** | Fresh basil | handful |

### Method
1. **Sauté** – Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion 3 min, then the garlic and red pepper flakes 1 min more.
2. **Dump** – Add the tomatoes, broth, dry pasta, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Stir so the pasta is submerged.
3. **Simmer** – Bring to a boil, then reduce to a strong simmer. Cook uncovered 12–14 min, stirring often, until the pasta is al dente and the sauce has thickened around it.
4. **Finish** – Off heat, stir in the cream and Parmesan until glossy.
5. **Serve** – Tear basil over the top. Nothing to drain, nothing to strain.

### Grocery List
- **Pasta**: 12 oz penne or rigatoni
- **Canned**: 1 can crushed tomatoes (28 oz), chicken or vegetable broth
- **Produce**: 1 onion, garlic, fresh basil
- **Dairy**: Heavy cream, Parmesan, butter
- **Seasoning**: Italian seasoning, red pepper flakes, salt, pepper
