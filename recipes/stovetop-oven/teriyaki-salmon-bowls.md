## 🍱 Teriyaki Salmon Bowls

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 20 min (stovetop)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Salmon fillets, skin removed | 4 (6 oz each) |
| **Teriyaki Sauce** | Soy sauce | ¼ cup |
| | Honey | 3 Tbsp |
| | Rice vinegar | 1 Tbsp |
| | Sesame oil | 1 tsp |
| | Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
| | Fresh ginger, grated | 1 tsp |
| | Cornstarch | 1 tsp |
| | Water | 1 Tbsp |
| **Bowl Base** | White or brown rice | 2 cups dry |
| **Toppings** | Edamame, shelled | 1 cup |
| | Shredded carrots | 1 cup |
| | Cucumber, sliced | 1 |
| | Green onions, sliced | 3 |
| | Sesame seeds | 1 Tbsp |

### Method
1. **Cook Rice** – Prepare rice according to package directions.
2. **Make Sauce** – Whisk together soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, and ginger in a small saucepan over medium heat. Stir together cornstarch and water, then add to the sauce. Simmer 2–3 min until slightly thickened. Set aside.
3. **Cook Salmon** – Heat a non-stick skillet over medium-high. Add a splash of oil. Cook salmon fillets 3–4 min per side until cooked through and lightly browned. Break into large chunks.
4. **Glaze** – Pour half the teriyaki sauce over the salmon in the pan, tossing gently to coat.
5. **Assemble Bowls** – Divide rice into bowls. Top with glazed salmon, edamame, carrots, and cucumber.
6. **Finish** – Drizzle remaining sauce over bowls. Top with green onions and sesame seeds.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 4 salmon fillets (~1.5 lb total)
- **Grains**: 2 cups white or brown rice
- **Produce**: cucumber, shredded carrots, green onions, fresh ginger, garlic
- **Frozen**: shelled edamame
- **Pantry**: soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, sesame oil, cornstarch, sesame seeds
