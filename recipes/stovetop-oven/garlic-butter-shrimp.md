## 🍤 Garlic Butter Shrimp

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 10 min (stovetop)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Large shrimp, peeled & deveined | 1.5 lb |
| **Flavor** | Butter | 4 Tbsp |
| | Garlic, minced | 5 cloves |
| | White wine or chicken broth | ¼ cup |
| | Lemon juice | 2 Tbsp |
| | Red pepper flakes | ¼ tsp |
| **Garnish** | Fresh parsley, chopped | 2 Tbsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |

### Method
1. **Prep Shrimp** – Pat shrimp dry and season with salt and pepper.
2. **Cook Shrimp** – Melt 2 Tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add shrimp in a single layer, cook 1–2 min per side until pink. Remove to a plate.
3. **Make Sauce** – Add remaining butter and garlic to skillet. Cook 30 sec. Add wine, lemon juice, and red pepper flakes. Simmer 1–2 min.
4. **Combine** – Return shrimp to skillet, toss to coat.
5. **Serve** – Garnish with parsley. Serve over rice, pasta, or with crusty bread.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1.5 lb large shrimp (peeled, deveined)
- **Dairy**: 1 stick butter
- **Produce**: Garlic, lemon, fresh parsley
- **Pantry**: White wine or chicken broth, red pepper flakes
