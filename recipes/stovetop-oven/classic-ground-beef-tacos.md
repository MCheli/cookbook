## 🌮 Classic Ground Beef Tacos

**Servings:** 4–6 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 15 min (stovetop)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Ground beef | 1 lb |
| **Seasoning** | Taco seasoning | 1 packet (or 2 Tbsp homemade) |
| | Water | ½ cup |
| **Shells** | Hard or soft taco shells | 8–12 |
| **Toppings** | Shredded lettuce | 2 cups |
| | Shredded cheddar cheese | 1 cup |
| | Diced tomatoes | 1 cup |
| | Sour cream | ½ cup |
| | Salsa | ½ cup |
| | *Optional* – Jalapeños, onion, cilantro | to taste |

### Method
1. **Brown Meat** – Cook ground beef in a skillet over medium-high heat for 5–7 min, breaking it up until no pink remains. Drain excess fat.
2. **Season** – Add taco seasoning and water. Stir and simmer 5 min until sauce thickens.
3. **Warm Shells** – Heat taco shells according to package directions.
4. **Assemble** – Fill shells with seasoned beef and top with your favorites.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1 lb ground beef
- **Seasoning**: Taco seasoning packet
- **Shells**: Hard or soft taco shells (8–12 count)
- **Produce**: Lettuce, tomatoes, jalapeños (optional), cilantro (optional)
- **Dairy**: Shredded cheddar, sour cream
- **Other**: Salsa
