## 🍜 Garlic Butter Noodles

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 15 min (stovetop)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Pasta** | Spaghetti or linguine | 1 lb |
| **Sauce** | Butter | 4 Tbsp |
| | Garlic, minced | 5 cloves |
| | Parmesan cheese, grated | ¾ cup |
| | Olive oil | 2 Tbsp |
| **Seasoning** | Salt & black pepper | to taste |
| | Red pepper flakes | ¼ tsp (optional) |
| | Fresh parsley, chopped | 2 Tbsp (optional) |
| **Pasta Water** | Reserved | ½ cup |

### Method
1. **Cook Pasta** – Boil pasta in well-salted water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water before draining.
2. **Toast Garlic** – While pasta cooks, melt butter with olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add garlic and cook 2–3 min until fragrant and just golden. Don't let it burn.
3. **Combine** – Add drained pasta to the skillet and toss to coat. Add pasta water a splash at a time until the sauce is silky.
4. **Finish** – Remove from heat, stir in parmesan until melted. Add red pepper flakes if using.
5. **Serve** – Top with parsley and extra parmesan.

### Grocery List
- **Pasta**: 1 lb spaghetti or linguine
- **Dairy**: butter, parmesan cheese (wedge or pre-grated)
- **Produce**: garlic, fresh parsley (optional)
- **Pantry**: olive oil, red pepper flakes
