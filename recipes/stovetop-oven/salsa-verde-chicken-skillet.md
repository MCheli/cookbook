## 🥑 Salsa Verde Chicken Skillet

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 25 min (one skillet with lid)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Boneless, skinless chicken thighs | 1.5–2 lb |
| **Seasoning** | Cumin | 1 tsp |
| | Chili powder | 1 tsp |
| | Salt | ¾ tsp |
| | Vegetable oil | 1 Tbsp |
| **Sauce** | Jarred salsa verde | 1 jar (16 oz) |
| | Black beans, drained & rinsed | 1 can (15 oz) |
| **Cheese** | Pepper jack or Monterey Jack, shredded | 1 cup |
| **Serving** | Warm tortillas or microwave rice pouches | for 4 |
| | Sour cream | ½ cup |
| | Fresh cilantro | handful |
| | Lime, cut into wedges | 1 |

### Method
1. **Season** – Pat the chicken dry and season both sides with the cumin, chili powder, and salt.
2. **Sear** – Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Sear the thighs 3–4 min per side until browned. They finish in the sauce, so don't worry about cooking them through.
3. **Simmer** – Pour the salsa verde and beans around the chicken. Bring to a simmer, then cover, drop to medium-low, and cook 10–12 min until the chicken reaches 165°F.
4. **Cheese** – Scatter the cheese over the top, cover, and let it melt 2 min.
5. **Serve** – Straight from the pan with tortillas or rice, sour cream, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime. For tacos, shred the chicken into the sauce with two forks.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 1.5–2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- **Pantry**: 1 jar salsa verde (16 oz), 1 can black beans, vegetable oil
- **Dairy**: 1 cup shredded pepper jack or Monterey Jack, sour cream
- **Produce**: cilantro, 1 lime
- **Spices**: cumin, chili powder, salt
- **Serving**: tortillas or microwave rice pouches
