## 🐟 Simple Baked Salmon

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 15 min (oven at 400°F)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Salmon fillets | 4 (6 oz each) |
| **Seasoning** | Garlic powder | 1 tsp |
| | Paprika | 1 tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |
| **Flavor** | Olive oil | 2 Tbsp |
| | Lemon slices | 1 lemon |
| | Fresh dill or parsley | 2 Tbsp (optional) |
| **Glaze (optional)** | Honey | 2 Tbsp |
| | Dijon mustard | 1 Tbsp |

### Method
1. **Preheat** – Heat oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with foil.
2. **Season** – Place salmon skin-side down. Drizzle with olive oil and season with garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper. Top with lemon slices.
3. **Optional Glaze** – Mix honey and Dijon, brush over fillets instead of dry seasonings.
4. **Bake** – Roast 12–15 min until salmon flakes easily with a fork.
5. **Serve** – Garnish with fresh herbs if desired.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 4 salmon fillets (about 1.5 lb total)
- **Produce**: 1 lemon, fresh dill or parsley (optional)
- **Spices**: Garlic powder, paprika, salt, pepper
- **Other**: Olive oil, honey, Dijon mustard (for optional glaze)
