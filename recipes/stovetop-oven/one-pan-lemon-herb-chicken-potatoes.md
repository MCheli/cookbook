## 🍳 One-Pan Lemon Herb Chicken & Potatoes

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 45 min (oven at 425°F)

### Ingredients
| Group | Item | Amount |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Protein** | Bone-in chicken thighs | 4–6 pieces |
| **Vegetables** | Baby potatoes, halved | 1.5 lb |
| | Green beans (optional) | 1 lb |
| **Seasoning** | Garlic, minced | 4 cloves |
| | Dried rosemary | 1 tsp |
| | Dried thyme | 1 tsp |
| | Salt & pepper | to taste |
| **Flavor** | Lemon, juiced | 1 |
| | Olive oil | 3 Tbsp |

### Method
1. **Preheat** – Heat oven to 425°F.
2. **Season** – Toss potatoes with 2 Tbsp olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper. Spread on a sheet pan.
3. **Add Chicken** – Rub chicken with remaining oil and seasonings. Nestle on top of potatoes.
4. **Bake** – Roast 35–45 min until chicken reaches 165°F and skin is crispy. Add green beans for the last 15 min if using.
5. **Finish** – Squeeze lemon juice over everything before serving.

### Grocery List
- **Protein**: 4–6 bone-in chicken thighs
- **Produce**: 1.5 lb baby potatoes, 1 lb green beans (optional), 1 lemon, garlic
- **Spices**: Rosemary, thyme, salt, pepper
- **Other**: Olive oil
